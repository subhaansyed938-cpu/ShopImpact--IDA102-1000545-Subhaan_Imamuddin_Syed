# 🌱 ShopImpact – Conscious Shopping Companion

### Your Purchases. Your Impact. A Greener Tomorrow.

ShopImpact is an interactive, Python-based sustainability application developed using **Streamlit**. It encourages users to become more conscious shoppers by helping them understand the estimated environmental impact of their everyday purchases.

The application provides a personalized shopping dashboard where users can record purchases, calculate estimated carbon emissions, monitor monthly spending, explore sustainable alternatives and earn achievements for adopting environmentally responsible shopping habits.

By combining interactive dashboards, real-time calculations, engaging visuals and personalized sustainability insights, ShopImpact transforms everyday shopping into an opportunity to make more informed and environmentally responsible decisions.

## 2. 💡 The Problem We Aim to Solve

Everyday shopping has an environmental impact that often goes unnoticed.

Many consumers consider the price, brand and quality of a product but rarely think about the carbon emissions associated with producing and purchasing it.

ShopImpact addresses three important challenges:

- **Lack of Awareness:** Consumers often do not understand the estimated carbon footprint of their purchases.
- **Limited Environmental Tracking:** People lack a simple way to monitor the environmental impact of their shopping habits.
- **Difficulty Finding Alternatives:** Identifying more sustainable products and brands can be challenging.

Our goal is to encourage awareness and positive behavioural changes without making sustainability feel complicated or overwhelming.

## 3. 🌿 Our Solution – ShopImpact

## 6. ♻️ Environmental Impact Calculation

ShopImpact uses predefined emission multipliers to estimate the environmental impact associated with recorded purchases.

The calculation follows the formula:

**Estimated CO₂ Impact = Product Price × Category Multiplier**

Each product category is assigned a multiplier representing its estimated environmental impact.

When a user records a purchase, the application calculates its estimated CO₂ impact and incorporates the result into the dashboard.

**Important:** These calculations are educational estimates based on predefined multipliers. They should not be interpreted as verified lifecycle carbon assessments of individual products.
